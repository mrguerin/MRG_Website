About

Finance Forged
in the Field

I'm a finance executive who came up through hotel operations — front desks, food and beverage, and reception across the U.S. and Europe — before moving into corporate finance, due diligence, and asset management. Thirteen years on, that path is the difference: I underwrite hotels with the instincts of someone who has actually run them.

At Aimbridge, I lead feasibility for a North American pipeline spanning new builds, conversions, and repositionings across every hotel tier — work that has generated $78M in new revenue and secured $40M in capital. Before that: financial due diligence at Hyatt's Corporate Transaction Group, M&A advisory at KPMG, and portfolio asset management under Blackstone. As a U.S./France dual citizen, I've worked both sides of the Atlantic — institutional finance in Chicago and Plano, luxury operations in France and California.

I completed a certificate in Artificial Intelligence at Columbia Business School Executive Education — and apply it directly to underwriting, due diligence, and market analysis. Fluent in English and French, working Spanish.
